02 What I make

A better way to make connections.

I create personalized NFC business cards that make sharing your details as simple as a tap. The card opens a digital profile on a compatible phone, where people can find your contact information, social links, and the details that matter to you.

It’s a small thing, but it makes a first meeting easier to remember.
