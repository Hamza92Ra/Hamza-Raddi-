# Asset sources

- `hamza.webp` — Hamza Raddi's portrait, downloaded from <https://hamza92ra.github.io/images/Hamza.jpg> and resized locally to 960 × 960 pixels.
- `fonts/dm-sans-latin.woff2` — DM Sans, from Google Fonts; licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `fonts/OFL-DM-Sans.txt`.
- `fonts/instrument-serif-italic-latin.woff2` — Instrument Serif, from Google Fonts; licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `fonts/OFL-Instrument-Serif.txt`.
- `fonts/noto-sans-arabic.woff2` and `fonts/noto-naskh-arabic.woff2` — Noto Sans Arabic and Noto Naskh Arabic, from Google Fonts; licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in their matching `OFL-*.txt` files.
- `instagram.svg`, `facebook.svg`, `linkedin.svg`, `github.svg`, `whatsapp.svg`, `telephone.svg`, `globe2.svg`, and `person-plus.svg` — Bootstrap Icons; licensed under the MIT License in `BOOTSTRAP-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`.
- `landing-preview-en.webp`, `landing-preview-fr.webp`, and `landing-preview-ar.webp` — local browser captures of this landing page in each language, optimized as WebP for the NFC simulation.
- `favicon.svg` — original monogram for this site.
